# Current Indian Railways / IRCTC reservation rules (as of 17 Nov 2026), with emphasis on 2025-2026 changes

Research note on method and reliability:
- Primary documents actually opened this session: one PIB Backgrounder PDF (6 Aug 2026). Railway Board circular texts and IRCTC's own notice pages were NOT opened; almost everything else rests on news reports of those circulars (PTI-syndicated outlets, Business Today, ETV Bharat, The Week, Hitavada, etc.).
- Items marked "(search snippet)" were seen only in search-result summaries, not on the fetched page. Treat them as leads to verify.
- Many third-party travel blogs carry "2026" in titles but only restate 2025 rules; several misdate rules (flagged below).
- No rule change dated September-November 2026 was found in any reliable source.

## 1. Tatkal and Premium Tatkal: timings, charges, limits, refunds, ID

### Takeaway
Tatkal still opens one day before journey (from the train's originating station) at 10:00 for AC classes and 11:00 for non-AC, max 4 passengers per PNR, confirmed Tatkal tickets non-refundable; no change to timing or charges was found in 2025-2026. What changed is access control: Aadhaar-authenticated accounts only (1 Jul 2025), OTP at booking (15 Jul 2025), and a 30-minute agent blackout.

### Cited Findings
- AC classes (2A/3A/3E/CC) open 10:00, Sleeper/2S open 11:00, one day before journey; guide updated 12 Nov 2026 says "Tatkal timings and charges remain the same" and lists no 2026 change — [EaseMyTrip guide, updated 12 Nov 2026](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- Tatkal charge: 10% of basic fare for 2S, 30% for all other classes, with min/max caps. One table: 2S Rs 10-15, SL Rs 100-200, CC Rs 125-225, 3A/3E Rs 300-400, 2A (and "1A" per that page) Rs 400-500 — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide). A different table seen in search results gives SL Rs 90-175, 3A Rs 250-350, 2A Rs 300-400 (search snippet; conflict unresolved, likely an older table) — [RailMitra](https://www.railmitra.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-timing-charges-rules)
- Max 4 passengers per Tatkal PNR; no senior-citizen or other concession in Tatkal — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- Confirmed Tatkal tickets: no refund on cancellation (refund only in special cases such as train cancellation/route change). The page is internally inconsistent on waitlisted Tatkal (one section: cancellable with deduction; FAQ: auto-cancelled and refunded if still waitlisted at charting) — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- In April 2025 IRCTC publicly denied viral claims that Tatkal timings were changing from 15 April 2025 (search snippet) — [Business Today, 11 Apr 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/new-tatkal-ticket-rules-from-april-15-heres-what-irctc-has-to-say-471774-2025-04-11)
- Premium Tatkal: same opening times (10:00 AC / 11:00 non-AC, one day before), only on selected trains, dynamic fare rising as seats fill, no RAC/waitlist tickets issued (confirmed only), agents cannot book at any time, refund rules same as Tatkal — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide); agents barred from Premium Tatkal also in [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/personal-finance/irctc-aadhaar-priority-booking-vs-tatkal-rules-what-s-different) (search snippet)
- Travel ID: carry an original photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, DL, PAN; DigiLocker versions accepted per the guide) — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- Counter Tatkal, 2026: Business Today (31 Jul 2026) reports a token-based system for Tatkal at PRS counters from 1 Aug 2026 (AC tokens 8:30-9:00, booking 10:00; non-AC tokens 9:30-10:00, booking 11:00; 10 AC / 15 non-AC tokens per window; online Tatkal "unchanged"). It cites no circular — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/india/story/planning-to-book-tatkal-tickets-indian-railways-august-1-rule-change-may-surprise-you-546275-2026-07-31). Another outlet says it is West Central Railway only initially and gives non-AC tokens as 9:00-9:30 (conflict), again unsourced — [Karmactive, 5 Aug 2026](https://www.karmactive.com/indian-railways-token-system-tatkal-2026/)

### Inferences
- For an online tool, the binding Tatkal constants remain: T-1 day, 10:00:00 IST (AC) / 11:00:00 IST (non-AC), <=4 passengers, no refund on confirmed. "One day before" is counted from the train's departure date at its originating station (EaseMyTrip words it as "from your boarding station"; the user brief and long-standing IRCTC rule say originating station — verify on IRCTC for overnight trains where the two dates differ).
- The counter token system (if real) does not affect online booking and appears zone-limited and weakly sourced.

### Gaps
- The official IRCTC/Indian Railways Tatkal charge table was not opened; the min/max rupee figures conflict across secondary sites.
- Could not confirm from an official source whether waitlisted Tatkal (TQWL) e-tickets are still issued everywhere and how the 60%/30% waitlist cap (section 6) applies in practice.
- Which trains/classes carry Premium Tatkal quota: no authoritative list found. Whether 1A/EC have Tatkal on any trains was not verified.
- Whether Premium Tatkal fare cap/refund has changed in 2026: no information found.

## 2. Aadhaar rules (Tatkal, opening-day general booking, counters, other ID tech)

### Takeaway
Online Tatkal is restricted to Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC accounts (since 1 Jul 2025) with OTP at booking (announced for 15 Jul 2025, actually rolled out train-by-train). For general (non-Tatkal) booking, the Aadhaar-only window on the ARP opening day grew from 15 minutes (1 Oct 2025) to the whole opening day, 08:00 to midnight (12 Jan 2026); no later change found.

### Cited Findings
- 1 Jul 2025: only Aadhaar-authenticated users can book Tatkal on IRCTC website/app; 15 Jul 2025: Aadhaar-based OTP authentication mandatory for online Tatkal; PRS-counter and agent Tatkal require an OTP sent to the mobile number given at booking — [News On AIR, 12 Jun 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/railways-announces-new-rules-for-tatkal-tickets-aadhaar-based-otp-authentication-must-from-july-1)
- PIB Backgrounder (6 Aug 2026) confirms: "Aadhaar-authenticated Tatkal booking introduced in July 2025"; "OTP-based verification added later to curb misuse by automated bots and unauthorised agents" — [PIB Backgrounder PDF](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc202686945401.pdf)
- Actual OTP rollout was phased by train: Lok Sabha reply Dec 2025 said Aadhaar OTP for online Tatkal was operational on 322 trains; counter OTP on 211 trains as of 4 Dec 2025; counter OTP pilot began 17 Nov 2025 and was quickly extended to 52 trains, with a stated plan to cover all remaining trains (search snippets) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/aadhaar-otp-verification-now-in-322-trains-for-online-tatkal-booking-30-million-fake-user-ids-busted-1.500376493), [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/india/news/aadhaar-otp-verification-now-in-322-trains-for-online-tatkal-booking-to-curb-misuse-and-improve-fairness-3-crore-fake-user-ids-busted-7235094.html/amp), [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/otp-must-for-tatkal-rly-tickets-at-counters)
- Non-Aadhaar users can still book Tatkal at PRS counters or via authorised agents with mobile-OTP verification — [EaseMyTrip, updated 12 Nov 2026](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- A Lok Sabha reply published 25 Mar 2026 reaffirmed that Tatkal on IRCTC website/app can be booked only by Aadhaar-authenticated users (search snippet, secondary site) — [TrainLiveStatus](https://trainlivestatus.com/blog/link-aadhaar-irctc-tatkal-booking)
- General reservation, opening day of ARP: from 1 Oct 2025 only Aadhaar-authenticated users could book in the first 15 minutes after opening (search snippet) — [Business Standard, 15 Sep 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/from-oct-train-booking-only-through-aadhar-for-first-15-mins-from-opening-125091501350_1.html)
- Phased extension (search snippets): 29 Dec 2025 Aadhaar-only until 12:00; 5 Jan 2026 Aadhaar-only 08:00-16:00; 12 Jan 2026 Aadhaar-only for the whole opening day (08:00 to midnight). ARP length unchanged — [Organiser, 29 Dec 2025](https://organiser.org/2025/12/29/332517/bharat/irctc-extends-ticket-booking-window-for-aadhaar-verified-users-phased-expansion-planned-till-midnight/), [ScanX](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/stocks/irctc-extends-aadhaar-verified-user-booking-window-to-midnight-from-january-12-2026/29774244), [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/irctc-extends-aadhaar-booking-slot-1929851)
- The Aadhaar opening-day rule applies to online booking; PRS counter timings for general tickets were stated as unchanged when the rule was introduced (search snippet) — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/national/only-aadhaar-authenticated-users-can-book-online-general-tickets-in-first-15-mins-from-oct-1-railways-846693)
- RailOne: Tatkal bookings require a verified Aadhaar or DigiLocker-linked account (search snippet, secondary) — [Smartprix](https://www.smartprix.com/bytes/a-guide-to-using-the-railone-app-how-to-guide/)

### Inferences
- A booking tool must assume: (a) the IRCTC account is Aadhaar-authenticated; (b) an Aadhaar OTP arrives on the Aadhaar-linked mobile during Tatkal booking and must be entered by the human account holder, so fully unattended Tatkal booking is not possible by design; (c) for a journey exactly 60 days out, a non-Aadhaar account cannot book online at all on the opening day.
- For short-notice travel (days before journey) the opening-day Aadhaar window is irrelevant; only the Tatkal Aadhaar/OTP rule bites.

### Gaps
- Whether Aadhaar OTP for online Tatkal now covers all trains (vs 322 in Dec 2025): no 2026 count found.
- No reliable report of face/biometric authentication for booking was found. DigiLocker appears only as an alternative verification path in secondary guides; not confirmed officially.
- Whether at least one passenger (not just the account) must be Aadhaar-verified for Tatkal: not confirmed this session.

## 3. Agent restrictions

### Takeaway
Authorised agents are locked out of Tatkal for the first 30 minutes (10:00-10:30 AC, 11:00-11:30 non-AC) since 1 Jul 2025 and cannot book Premium Tatkal at all; a 10-minute agent bar on opening-day general bookings is reported as continuing.

### Cited Findings
- Agents cannot book opening-day Tatkal 10:00-10:30 (AC) and 11:00-11:30 (non-AC), effective 1 Jul 2025 (search snippet) — [Deccan Herald explainer](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/explained-all-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-rules-for-tatkal-ticket-booking-3582881); reiterated in the 24 Mar 2026 ministerial briefing ("30-minute agent booking restriction at the start") — [ETV Bharat, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/indian-railways-revises-rules-for-refunds-on-cancellation-of-tickets-enn26032402810)
- Premium Tatkal: agents not permitted at any time — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)
- General reservation opening: "the ban on authorised agents booking tickets in the first 10 minutes will continue" (search snippet) — [Asianet Newsable](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/gallery/business/indian-railways-updates-tatkal-booking-rule-what-you-must-know-kcszklo)
- An older (c. 2018) report lists agent blackouts 08:00-08:30, 10:00-10:30, 11:00-11:30 (search snippet; conflicts with the 10-minute figure for general booking) — [Factly](https://factly.in/these-are-the-checks-put-in-place-to-prevent-misuse-of-tatkal-booking/amp)

### Inferences
- Since 12 Jan 2026 the whole ARP opening day online is Aadhaar-authenticated-users-only, which may make the agent 10-minute rule moot in practice; how agents are treated on opening day after 08:10 was not found.

### Gaps
- Exact current agent window for opening-day general booking (10 vs 30 minutes) not confirmed from an official source.

## 4. Advance Reservation Period (ARP)

### Takeaway
ARP remains 60 days (excluding journey date), in force since 1 Nov 2024; no change found through Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- ARP cut from 120 to 60 days effective 1 Nov 2024 — [News On AIR, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/new-railway-ticket-reservation-rules-come-into-effect-from-today)
- PIB Backgrounder (6 Aug 2026) lists "ARP reduced from 120 days to 60 days" as the standing rule — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc202686945401.pdf)
- A 7 Nov 2026 blog says the 60-day cut is "effective November 1, 2026" — this is a misdating of the 2024 change, with no circular cited; its own example (journey 7 Jan 2027 opens 08:00 on 8 Nov 2026) is consistent with a 60-day ARP — [HelloRail](https://hellorail.in/indian-railways-ticket-booking-rules-january-2027/)
- General booking opens 08:00 on the ARP opening day (same source) — [HelloRail](https://hellorail.in/indian-railways-ticket-booking-rules-january-2027/)

### Inferences
- None beyond the above.

### Gaps
- Exceptions (short-ARP day trains, 365-day foreign tourist quota) were not re-verified this session.

## 5. Chart preparation, second chart, current booking

### Takeaway
First chart moved from 4 hours to 8 hours before departure (mid-2025), then to roughly 10 hours / previous evening (journeys from 30 Dec 2025); the Ministry in March 2026 described this as charting "9-18 hours" before departure. A second/final chart is still made about 15-30 minutes before departure, and vacant berths after first chart are sold as current booking until then.

### Cited Findings
- Old rule: chart 4 hours before departure. Mid-2025 decision: 8 hours before; for trains leaving before 14:00, chart at 21:00 the previous night; phased rollout — [Telangana Today, 30 Jun 2025](https://telanganatoday.com/indian-railways-to-ease-travel-planning-with-early-chart-preparation)
- Dec 2025 revision (Railway Board Passenger Marketing circular), for journeys from 30 Dec 2025: departures 05:01-14:00 -> first chart by 20:00 the previous day; departures 14:01-23:59 and 00:00-05:00 -> first chart at least 10 hours before departure — [The Hitavada, 27 Dec 2025](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2025/12/27/now-railway-reservation-charts-to-be-ready-one-day-in-advance.html); 10-hour rule for those two bands "with immediate effect" also in [The Week, 18 Dec 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2025/12/18/new-railway-rules-check-rac-status-10-hours-before-journey.amp.html)
- 24 Mar 2026 ministry statement: advance charting "now happens 9-18 hours before departure instead of 4 hours" (blog quoting PIB PRID 2244597) — [Trayaan, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.trayaan.com/2026/03/indian-railways-announces-new-reforms-for-better-passenger-convenience-here-are-the-full-details.html)
- Same-day wire report phrases it as first chart "at least 8 hours before departure" and second chart "15 to 30 minutes before departure" — [ETV Bharat, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/indian-railways-revises-rules-for-refunds-on-cancellation-of-tickets-enn26032402810)
- Second chart is prepared 30 to 5 minutes before departure, mainly for current-booking passengers, per a railway official (search snippet) — [ThePrint, mid-2025](https://theprint.in/india/now-reservation-charts-at-9-pm-for-next-day-5-am-2-pm-trains-8-hours-in-advance-for-others/2677685)
- Current booking: unused seats can be booked after first chart, online, closing about 30 minutes before departure (wording on the page is unclear) — [EaseMyTrip](https://www.easemytrip.com/blog/tatkal-ticket-booking-guide)

### Inferences
- "9-18 hours" is arithmetically consistent with the Dec 2025 bands (a 05:01 train charted at 20:00 = ~9 h; a 14:00 train = 18 h; other bands 10 h).
- Implication for last-minute seats: waitlist/RAC outcome and released Tatkal/quota vacancies become visible ~10-18 hours before departure rather than 4. The current-booking (CURR_AVBL) window is therefore much longer than before — from first chart until the final chart (~30 min before departure) — and the best moment to poll is right after first-chart time (20:00 the previous evening for 05:01-14:00 departures; departure minus 10 h otherwise).
- Since no refund is given within 8 hours of departure (section 7), late cancellations after first chart return berths with no incentive to cancel early; some berths may still be released to current booking but many no-shows will simply not cancel.

### Gaps
- The Railway Board circular itself (number/date) was not opened. Hitavada's sentence about the "second chart... existing 8-hour window... at an intermediate station" appears to concern remote-location charting and was not clear; remote-location chart timing is unverified.
- Exact cut-off of online current booking (30 vs 15 vs 5 minutes before departure) not confirmed officially.

## 6. Waitlist, RAC, cancellation/refund, TDR

### Takeaway
Waitlist issuance is capped (25% from Apr 2025, revised 28 Jun 2025 to 60% of redefined capacity for AC and 30% for non-AC), and waitlisted passengers are barred from reserved coaches (1 May 2025). Cancellation slabs were tightened in April 2026 to 72/24/8 hours, with zero refund inside 8 hours.

### Cited Findings
- 17 Apr 2025 order capped waitlist at 25% of berths per class; superseded by circular of 28 Jun 2025: "maximum current waiting list limits will be revised to 60% and 30% of the redefined capacity of each class available at remote locations as well as tickets booked under Tatkal scheme" — 60% AC, 30% non-AC; not applicable to concessional fares/warrants (search snippets) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/railway-board-revises-25-cap-on-waiting-list-tickets-following-public-outcry), [The Week/PTI, 29 Jun 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/06/29/del48-rail-enhances-waiting-list.html). One outlier says 40% for sleeper/chair car — [Patrika](https://www.patrika.com/en/national-news/indian-railway-ticket-booking-new-rules-19780424) (search snippet; conflict)
- From 1 May 2025 waitlisted ticket holders (online or counter) may not travel in Sleeper/AC coaches; TTE can fine and move them to unreserved coaches; fines of Rs 250 (SL) / Rs 440 (AC) plus fare are reported by one source only (search snippets) — [The Week, 1 May 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2025/05/01/indian-railway-5-rule-changes-may-1-waiting-list-tickets-not-allowed-sleeper-ac-coach-irctc-app-cancellation-fee-refund-rule-and-more.html), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/asia/india/indian-railways-announces-tougher-travel-rules-new-reservation-guidelines-and-penalties-1.500115332)
- Sleeper tickets no longer auto-upgraded to First AC (2025) (search snippet) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/big-change-in-indian-railways-waiting-tickets-will-no-longer-be-upgraded-to-first-ac)
- New refund slabs for confirmed tickets (notification dated 16 Jan 2026 amending the Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket and Refund of Fare) Rules, 2015; announced 24 Mar 2026; effective 1 Apr 2026 per Business Today, "April 1 to 15, 2026" per ETV Bharat; already applied to Vande Bharat Sleeper and Amrit Bharat II trains launched after Jan 2026): more than 72 h before departure = flat charge only; 72-24 h = 25% deduction; 24-8 h = 50% deduction; under 8 h = no refund. Old slabs were 48/12/4 h — [ETV Bharat, 24 Mar 2026](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/indian-railways-revises-rules-for-refunds-on-cancellation-of-tickets-enn26032402810), [Business Today, 28 Mar 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/before-you-cancel-your-train-ticket-check-these-new-refund-rules-by-indian-railways-522453-2026-03-28)
- Flat cancellation charges (>72 h), per passenger, GST extra on AC: 1A/EC Rs 240; 2A/FC Rs 200; 3A/CC/3E Rs 180; SL Rs 120; 2S Rs 60 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/before-you-cancel-your-train-ticket-check-these-new-refund-rules-by-indian-railways-522453-2026-03-28)
- Waitlist/RAC tickets "follow the older protocols": cancel up to 30 minutes before departure with clerkage of about Rs 60 per passenger — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/before-you-cancel-your-train-ticket-check-these-new-refund-rules-by-indian-railways-522453-2026-03-28)
- TDR: requirement to file a TDR for e-tickets removed "in many cases", refunds processed automatically on cancellation — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/before-you-cancel-your-train-ticket-check-these-new-refund-rules-by-indian-railways-522453-2026-03-28), [Trayaan/PIB summary](https://www.trayaan.com/2026/03/indian-railways-announces-new-reforms-for-better-passenger-convenience-here-are-the-full-details.html)
- Counter tickets can be cancelled at any station counter (reported as effective from 1 Apr 2026 by Business Today; "soon" by the PIB-derived summary) — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/before-you-cancel-your-train-ticket-check-these-new-refund-rules-by-indian-railways-522453-2026-03-28), [Trayaan](https://www.trayaan.com/2026/03/indian-railways-announces-new-reforms-for-better-passenger-convenience-here-are-the-full-details.html)

### Inferences
- With the waitlist cap, "REGRET/no more booking" is reached sooner; a tool should treat a short waitlist as normal and not expect deep WL numbers.
- A waitlisted ticket is no longer a travel fallback (cannot board reserved coaches), so only CNF/RAC outcomes are usable.
- Speculative "book and cancel later" strategies are now expensive: cancelling 8-24 h out costs 50%, under 8 h costs 100%.

### Gaps
- Auto-cancellation and full refund of fully waitlisted e-tickets at charting, and RAC rules (RAC holders may board; cancellation up to 30 min before departure) are long-standing rules that were not re-verified against an official source this session.
- Whether the 72/24/8 slabs apply uniformly to all trains as of Nov 2026 (the "April 1-15" phasing wording is ambiguous).
- Exact scope of "TDR removed" (e.g. train late >3 h, AC failure, partial travel claims) not found.

## 7. Per-user limits, master list, anti-bot checks

### Takeaway
Monthly cap is 12 tickets per IRCTC user ID, 24 if Aadhaar-linked (with at least one Aadhaar-verified passenger on the extra tickets). Railways report aggressive anti-bot enforcement (Akamai filtering, ~3 crore IDs deactivated), but no current, officially stated numeric timing checks were found.

### Cited Findings
- 12 tickets/month for non-Aadhaar-linked IDs, 24/month for Aadhaar-linked IDs, the higher limit requiring at least one Aadhaar-verified passenger on the ticket (rule dates from June 2022; restated in Jan 2026 coverage) (search snippets) — [National Herald/PTI 2022](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/booking-up-to-24-train-tickets-a-month-allowed-if-irctc-user-id-linked-to-aadhaar), [PuneNow, 13 Jan 2026](https://www.punenow.com/?p=52929)
- About 3.02-3.03 crore suspicious user IDs deactivated since Jan 2025; Akamai anti-bot tooling in use; 60.43 billion malicious bot requests blocked in six months to Dec 2025 (Rajya Sabha reply, 6 Feb 2026) (search snippets) — [Zee Business](https://www.zeebiz.com/indian-railways/news-aadhaar-otp-system-boosts-tatkal-ticket-availability-and-strengthens-cybersecurity-railways-385477), [NewKerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/train-tickets-over-crore-suspicious-user-ids-deactivated-303.htm)
- Older (c. 2018) checks reported: minimum ~25 s on passenger form, 5 s minimum for captcha on passenger and payment pages (another outlet: 20 s form + 20 s payment); only two Tatkal tickets per user ID/IP between 10:00 and 12:00; captcha at login, passenger and payment pages (search snippets; may be outdated) — [Factly](https://factly.in/these-are-the-checks-put-in-place-to-prevent-misuse-of-tatkal-booking/amp), [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/india/railways-issues-new-rules-for-irctc-ticket-booking/story)
- 2025 report: payment timeout extended from 3 to 5 minutes and captcha simplified (search snippet) — [Business Today, Apr 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/new-tatkal-ticket-rules-from-april-15-heres-what-irctc-has-to-say-471774-2025-04-11)
- PRS scale: 5.72 crore+ online user accounts, 5 lakh+ concurrent users/sessions, 89% of reserved tickets booked online (Jun 2025-Jun 2026) — [PIB Backgrounder, 6 Aug 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/aug/doc202686945401.pdf)

### Inferences
- Scripted form-filling or request automation risks ID deactivation; a compliant tool should assist a human (alerts, pre-filled master list, timing) rather than submit programmatically.

### Gaps
- No current official source for: master-list size limit, single-session/single-login enforcement, one-booking-per-session in 08:00-12:00, or login-time restrictions around 10:00/11:00. These exist in IRCTC lore and older FAQs but were not verifiable this session.
- Current minimum-time thresholds are not published; the 2018 numbers should not be coded as fact.

## 8. Boarding point change, name change, date change/reschedule

### Takeaway
Boarding point can now be changed digitally up to 30 minutes before departure (announced 24 Mar 2026; reported effective April 2026), instead of only before chart preparation. Free online date-change/rescheduling was announced in Oct 2025 for January 2026 but is not confirmed live; a Nov 2026-updated guide says IRCTC still does not offer it.

## 9. Quota types/codes and intermediate-station allocation
