Each result opens a booking sheet with the exact values and steps. railseek never asks for your login and never books or pays.

Availability comes from an unofficial data source and can be out of date or wrong. Always confirm on IRCTC before paying. Timings follow reported 2025-26 rules and are a guide.

railseek is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with IRCTC or Indian Railways, does not sell tickets, and does not ask for or store any login, passenger or payment details. Live figures are saved copies from a third-party source, each shown with the time it was saved; demo data shows invented trains.
